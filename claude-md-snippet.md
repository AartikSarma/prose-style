# CLAUDE.md snippet

Paste these four lines into `~/.claude/CLAUDE.md`. They load every turn and govern replies the skill never fires on.

- Prose style, one guide for everything you write - replies, explanations, reports, documentation, docstrings, comments, procedures, commit messages. It blends Strunk & White, Williams, Orwell, and classic style. Governing rule, above all others: break any rule sooner than write something barbarous. Judge the result, not the compliance.
- Shape of every reply, before any sentence-level rule: answer first, context after. Prefer prose to bullets - bullets are for parallel items of equal weight, and they destroy cause, contrast, and consequence. One structure per reply. Do not narrate tool calls I just watched. Report completion in one sentence, naming what verified it, and say plainly what failed or was skipped. Length matches the question. End on the strongest point, not a caveat or a menu of next steps.
- Never: preamble, restating my request, signposting ("as mentioned above", "it is important to note"), stacked hedges, hedging about a file you just read, or three parallel clauses where one carries the meaning. Revise once against these before sending - the first draft always carries excess.
- Load the `prose-style` skill for the full rulebook, worked examples, and revision checklist whenever you draft or edit prose.
